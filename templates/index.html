The missing number

Do you know what your wardrobe is worth?

Most people can name what they bought recently. Far fewer can say what they already own, what they actually use, what sits unworn, or what could help pay for something new.

Banqed gives you the system to find out. Reset your wardrobe section by section. Lodge each item once. Then see the patterns: value, use, source, style, resale willingness and sales. It is not about selling everything. It is about knowing what is there before you buy more, sell, donate, repair or wear again.

How it works

Businesses know what they own, what it's worth, and what they should sell. Banqed gives you the same, for your wardrobe.

  1. 01 Open your banq account

    Go through your wardrobe one section at a time and lodge each piece: its value, how often you wear it, where it came from.

  2. 02 Understand

    The system reads what you've lodged and shows you the patterns: what you actually wear, what sits unused, what's worth selling. This is the banq account you always owned but could never see the value of.

  3. 03 Resell

    Now you can decide what's worth keeping. Move anything you're done with into the sales pipeline, list it, sell it, and track what you make.

P.05 Circulation
banqn.
A personal record of owned items, organised by value, use, source and resale willingness. Takes the category as a prefix. Ex. wardrobe banq, book banq.
banqedadj.
Counted, valued and made visible. Ex. a banqed wardrobe.
banqern.
Someone who knows what they own, knows what it's worth, and acts on it.
banqingv.
Opening a banq, understanding what you own, and circulating it back into use.
circulationn.
The movement of belongings back into use through wearing, repairing, lending, donating, selling or renting.
dormant valuen.
Value held in items that are owned but rarely or never used.
lodgementn.
An item added to a banq, with its details recorded: source, value, use, rating and resale willingness.
resale willingnessn.
A personal measure of how open the owner is to selling an item. Scale: keep; keep for now; maybe sell; sell if price is right; sell now.
listed valuen.
The combined value of items currently listed for sale.
